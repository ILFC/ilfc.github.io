ILFC

Interactions entre Linguistiques Formelles et Computationnelles

Formal × computational linguistics

Monthly online seminar

The ILFC seminar, organised by RT LIFT2, brings together researchers from formal and computational linguistics, across the communities and around the world, to share their perspectives. It takes place online, roughly once a month during the academic year (September to June). Attendance is free. The seminar gave rise to BriGap (Bridges and Gaps between Formal and Computational Linguistics, brigap-workshop.github.io), a series of workshops where linguists and NLP scientists meet.

Join the mailing list to get the Zoom link and announcements.

About

The seminar is organised by the thematic network RT LIFT2 (Linguistique informatique, formelle et de terrain), previously the GdR LIFT. It started in 2021 and has welcomed speakers from linguistics, natural language processing and cognitive science.
