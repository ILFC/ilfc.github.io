# ILFC, Interaction between Formal and Computational Linguistics

RT LIFT2 (previously “GdR LIFT”) is organising a monthly online seminar on the interactions between formal and computational linguistics.
The seminar is intended to make members of diverse scientific communities around the world meet and share their different perspectives.
It takes place online, roughly once a month from September to June. Attendance is free. Join the [mailing list](https://sympa.inria.fr/sympa/subscribe/seminaire_ilfc) to get the Zoom link and announcements.

The seminar gave rise to BriGap (Bridges and Gaps between Formal and Computational Linguistics, [brigap-workshop.github.io](https://brigap-workshop.github.io/)).
